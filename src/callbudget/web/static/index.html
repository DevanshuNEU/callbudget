Active sensing under a calling budget

CallBudget

Pharmacy medication search framed as active sensing under a calling budget: predict which pharmacy most likely has a hard-to-find drug, call the most-likely ones first, and learn from every call.

Real scaffolding, synthetic outcomes. The pharmacies (NPPES), the drug identity (RxNorm), and the Adderall shortage window are real; the per-call in-stock outcomes are simulated, because real-time shelf-stock data is exactly what does not exist anywhere. That gap is the premise.
